# StudyPath 0.1.6 — Tide Play

Phone-testing prerelease prepared **5 November 2026**. [Download StudyPath.apk](https://github.com/marina21-cs/StudyPath/releases/download/v0.1.6/StudyPath.apk) and install it as an update, keeping your original account and app data.

## Changes

- Tide blue actions, sun/peach/mint/lilac subject surfaces, native subject tiles and compact PDF controls.
- Connected Study/Saved/Planner personal subjects, materials, grades and prerequisite records.
- PDF/image COR import: choose a page, adjust the course-name crop, recognize on-device, correct names, review and add selected subjects. Rotation and a numeric crop editor are included. Codes and units are not inferred.
- Wikipedia suggestions based on reviewed course names. PDF/module discovery opens browser search; no automatic download or school approval is implied.
- StudyPath giraffe on welcome/onboarding, a compact Study greeting, empty subject/material guidance and short correct-practice feedback. Motion respects disabled Android animations.

See the [design contract](../design/tide-play.md) for the reference translation, palette and mascot map.
