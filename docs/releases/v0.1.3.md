# StudyPath 0.1.3 — Personal Workspace

Released **4 November 2026** as a phone-testing prerelease. Install [StudyPath.apk](https://github.com/marina21-cs/StudyPath/releases/download/v0.1.3/StudyPath.apk) as an update to the previous compatible installation. Keep your original account and app data.

## Changes

- Personal subjects, tasks with optional dates, authored study cards, and PDF imports.
- A native PDF reader with page navigation and retained reading position.
- Full Wikipedia article text in selectable, navigable pages, alongside the existing bounded saved excerpt.
- A dedicated profile-name editor, clearer Account controls, and compact navigation/browse choices.
- Personal organization remains available when the approved course catalog is empty.

Personal content stays on this phone and is not cloud backed up or shared as official academic results. Uninstalling or clearing app data removes it. Personal task dates do not schedule notifications. PDFs are limited to 25 MiB and 500 pages each, with a 250 MiB total per account. Full articles require a successful online fetch; full-article offline retention is not guaranteed.
