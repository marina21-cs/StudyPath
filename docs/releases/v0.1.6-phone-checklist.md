# StudyPath 0.1.6 phone test

Install as an update over your existing StudyPath app and keep the same account. Do not clear data or uninstall to work around a signing conflict.

- Check the giraffe on welcome/onboarding, the small Study greeting, empty subject/material screens and correct practice feedback. Check that turning Android animations off keeps a stable character.
- Open Study, Saved, Planner and Account. Check the Tide blue controls, readable dark text and colored subject cards.
- Open Study → All subjects. Check the subject tiles and the single-column layout with larger Android font settings.
- In Planner → Import COR, choose a PDF or image. Move the blue corners around course names only, or use **Edit crop percentages**. Rotate the page or choose a different PDF page when needed.
- Tap **Read selected course names**. Correct every name; remove column headings and student information. Join wrapped lines. Codes and units are not inferred by OCR.
- Review the selected names and confirm. Verify that subjects appear once and existing subjects/materials are preserved.
- Confirm that Wikipedia suggestions load for a selected course. **Search the web for PDFs & modules** opens the browser; it does not auto-download or certify results.
- Repeat the crop/read step offline: bundled Latin-script OCR should still operate. Online suggestions should show a recoverable connection message.
- Resume an existing saved reading and reopen an existing local PDF. Check your notes and reading place after updating.

Real institutional COR variation, device/provider-specific image orientation, prolonged background/process death, broad TalkBack/200% text coverage and production cloud acceptance require phone testing. Keep original COR/PDF files. Personal subjects and PDFs remain on-device, without cloud backup.
