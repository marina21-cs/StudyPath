# StudyPath feedback recovery plan · 6 November 2026

Goal: make academic tools visible and usable, deliver a real prerequisite map, clean Wikipedia readings, and save PDFs directly from links. Audit baseline: 0.1.6 / commit 72b0a585. This is a proposed plan, not completed implementation.

- [ ] **1 — Surface the tools.** Put Grades & Scores, GWA, What-if and Prerequisite Map at the top of Planner, before subject/task lists. Show useful setup/pending/available states even with an empty catalog. Add a Grades shortcut to subject cards. Verify every destination is reachable within two taps and does not disappear when policy is missing.
- [ ] **2 — Fix Wikipedia extraction.** Extract article-body blocks in MediaWikiClient through a dedicated parser; exclude infobox/sidebar/navigation/references, preserve headings/lists and handle equations explicitly. Use the captured DeepSeek revision plus fixtures for math, tables and nested lists. Verify no sidebar topic dump, citation debris or duplicate blocks in excerpt/full reader; preserve attribution and existing saved notes/place during opt-in clean-copy refresh.
- [ ] **3 — Add PDF-link intake.** Materials → Add material → Paste PDF link / Import file; also receive Android Share text/PDF URIs. Use bounded streaming download, validated redirects/PDF bytes, progress/cancel/retry, subject assignment and private atomic storage. Verify a real link opens offline after restart, with failure/cancellation leaving existing files intact.
- [ ] **4 — Connect personal subjects to academic plans.** Add explicit mapping to a reviewed catalog course/term/template; do not match by title alone. Keep entered final grades distinct from assessment scores and hypothetical overrides. Verify old subject IDs, PDFs and records survive mapping and updates.
- [ ] **5 — Make grading-template review usable.** Add per-subject syllabus/template setup and Draft → Awaiting review → Approved → Outdated states, with weights, aggregation, scale, units, missing/excused and retake/rounding rules. For the first pilot use real professor review recorded and published by a maintainer; students cannot self-approve. Verify exact version/provenance and revoked/stale handling. An in-app professor portal is a separate proposed scope extension.
- [ ] **6 — Expose existing calculations.** Reuse GradeEngine, AcademicGwa and AcademicScenarios with dedicated score, GWA and scenario screens. Explain missing inputs and policy; do not bypass academic guards. Verify zero vs ungraded, weighted scores, included attempts/retakes, target feasibility and stale scenarios with independent expected values; scenarios must not overwrite actuals.
- [ ] **7 — Deliver the node map.** Render course nodes and connecting edges, explicit AND/OR groups, blockers, unknowns and selected-course details; provide an equivalent accessible list. Personal links remain labeled unreviewed and never infer eligibility. Verify multi-branch chains, cycle rejection, missing nodes and large-text/TalkBack behavior.
- [ ] **8 — Verify and release last.** Run focused parser/download/domain tests, changed Firebase-rule tests, migration checks and feature-specific light-emulator flows. Record exact source/APK/signature and observed results before publishing another testing APK. Real applicable syllabus/scale/prerequisite review is required before claiming academic acceptance.

Delivery order: 1–3 are the first visible repair batch; 4–6 complete the academic path once real policy evidence is available; 7 depends on 4 and can display clearly labeled personal links earlier. Step 8 validates each shipped batch. Detailed findings and sources: [feedback audit](docs/implementation/2026-11-06-feedback-audit.md).
